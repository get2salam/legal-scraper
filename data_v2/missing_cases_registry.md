## Patterns & Analysis

### Why are cases missing?

All 49 missing cases share the same pattern: PLS includes them in search results (with a citation and case ID), but when the scraper tries to fetch the actual judgment content, PLS returns an error or empty response. This is a **PLS server-side issue**, not a scraper bug.

### Reporter patterns:

- **PTD** (16 missing, 33%): Pakistan Tax Decisions -- worst affected. Tax tribunal cases may have upload delays or access restrictions.
- **MLD** (12 missing, 24%): All from 2025. Likely a recent batch that hasn't been fully uploaded yet.
- **CLD** (9 missing, 18%): 8 of 9 are from 2021 Competition Commission of Pakistan. Likely never properly added.
- **SCMR** (0 missing): 100% coverage. Supreme Court cases are best maintained on PLS.
